About Me

Hello, and welcome to caseymckenzie.online. I am Casey McKenzie and I am a teacher at the Springfield High School of Science and Technology in Springfield, MA. I provide students with the opportunity to learn how to build real websites, apps, and creative projects using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also enjoy getting an opportunity to work with students in an athletic environment through sports coaching as the school's Head Baseball Coach and Head Boys Soccer Coach. In the Spring of 2024 I launched the school's Student Council with a goal to promote student voice and I currently facilitate that club.
